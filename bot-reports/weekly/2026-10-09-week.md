## Research (mechanism, not slogans)

1. **Midday liquidity and momentum** — Intraday volume tends to be **U-shaped** (high at open/close, thin midday). Traders with discretion over timing cluster activity when others are present (**Admati & Pfleiderer, 1988**, summarized in [DayTradingToolkit — Chop and Pop](https://daytradingtoolkit.com/strategies/the-chop-and-pop-strategy-afternoon-reversals)). Breakouts during thin liquidity lack follow-through because there are fewer participants to sustain the move ([Thinkorswim lunch research](https://tosindicators.com/research/lunch-trading): momentum strategies fight the environment; mean reversion fits better when volume is low).

2. **Practitioner mapping** — [Investing.com intraday rhythm guide](https://www.investing.com/analysis/market-timing-how-intraday-rhythms-shape-stock-trades-200667883) labels **11:30–13:30 ET** as **midday chop** where trend continuation is low quality and recommends avoiding momentum-style trades there.

3. **VWAP fade quality** — Mean reversion toward VWAP is only well-defined on a **session** anchor. Fading stretch on a **noisy micro VWAP** (15-second print-count bars) fires in chop without a true extension; the lunch-hour write-up above notes that **VWAP deviations during 11:30–13:00** mean-revert more often than they trend — fading requires **extreme** stretch, not every tick above a twitchy average.

## Change made (and why it targets the failure)

### 1. `VWAP_REVERSION` — session anchor + stricter stretch

- The trainer engine now passes **`sessionVwap`** from **1-minute** session bars (same math as the trend gate) into strategy evaluation.
- Reversion fades use that anchor (not 15-second print VWAP), require **1.5× ATR** stretch (was **1×**), and require a **wick through the band** plus close still on the stretched side — fewer marginal fades in noise.

Shared helper: `sessionVwapFromCandles` in `bot-strategy.util.ts` (trend gate delegates to it).

### 2. `EMA_MOMENTUM` — lunch blackout

- No new signals **11:30–13:00 ET** (bar timestamp). This directly covers the **12:00** bucket where **CALL** momentum lost **−$1,105.80** while leaving the **13:00** bucket (where **PUT** momentum was **+$874**) intact.

No cooldown, contract filters, or kill-switch changes. Personal bots only see this if they enable these strategies.

## What would falsify this

1. **`VWAP_REVERSION × PUT`** remains the largest loser over the next full week despite session-anchored fades (would argue disabling the rule on the trainer, not further tuning).
2. **`EMA_MOMENTUM × CALL`** losses **shift into 13:00–14:00** with similar magnitude (blackout too narrow — would widen or drop the rule).
3. **Trade count collapses** with no P&L gain (filters too tight — relax stretch from 1.5× toward 1.25× ATR while keeping session VWAP).
4. **`rapidScalpReplay` delta vs actual** shrinks toward zero while entry P&L stays negative (would reopen exit work with a different lever than `premiumStopPct`).
