# Weekly improvement — v2|SPY|60 (week ending 2026-10-02)

## Failure identified

Two entry problems and one exit problem drove the week’s negative expectancy despite high win rates.

**1. ORB re-fire churn.** `ORB_5M` signals on every bar whose close remains outside the opening range, so after each two-minute cooldown the same breakout can re-enter. In the dossier, `ORB_5M` **CALL** at **10:00** was **5 trades, −$374, 40% WR** — the worst strategy×window cell for an enabled rule. Replay with entity defaults (`VWAP_PULLBACK` + `ORB_5M`, 2-minute cooldown) produced **127 trades, −$629 net, 66% WR** but **avg win $54 vs avg loss $119** (2.2:1 loss asymmetry). **22** exits were `UNDERLYING_STOP` (−$2,711) and **15** were `PREMIUM_STOP` (−$2,286); trail and target exits were net positive.

**2. Champion bleed into the house trainer.** One-time seeding copied the retired champion’s `strategiesEnabled`, which added `EMA_MOMENTUM`, `VWAP_REVERSION`, and `VWAP_ROLLING_100`. Those rules dominated live losses: **EMA_MOMENTUM CALL −$1,640** (38 trades), **VWAP_REVERSION CALL −$1,274** (38 trades), while `VWAP_PULLBACK` and `ORB_5M` were smaller drags. Adding champion strategies to the same replay window worsened net P&L from **−$629 to −$1,005** on identical bars.

**3. Premium target too far for 0DTE capture.** Median capture efficiency was **0.29**; `rapidScalpReplay` on 2026-10-02 scored **42** trades at **−$878** vs actual **−$1,704** (+$826) with a fee-aware ~1% take-profit — exit-side only, same entries. Lowering `premiumTargetPct` from 22 to 15 on the baseline rule set improved replay from **−$629 to −$202** without changing entries.

Session totals: **−$2,171 / −$30 / −$1,704** across three days; **130 trades**, aggregate win rate **~64%**, still deeply red.

## Research

- **One-shot ORB vs sustained-outside-range signals.** `evaluateOrb5mCrossover` (already in `bot-strategy.util.ts`) requires the prior bar inside the range and the current bar outside — the standard “break and hold” gate described for intraday breakouts ([SpotGamma 0DTE guide](https://spotgamma.com/0dte/): wait for confirmation, do not re-fire every bar still outside the level).
- **Take profit before gamma/theta take it back.** 0DTE long premium faces accelerating theta into the close; practitioners commonly bank partial gains rather than hold for full target ([OptionsDecay 0DTE guide](https://www.optionsdecay.com/0dte-options-trading/): close a meaningful fraction of max profit; [Ticker Daily 0DTE](https://tickerdaily.com/learn/options/0dte): gamma can flip a small underlying dip into a large premium loss). That matches the dossier’s low capture efficiency and the `rapidScalpReplay` uplift.
- **Precedent in-repo.** `BotV2RapidProfileService` already maps `ORB_5M` → `ORB_5M_CROSS` for the SPXW profile — the crossover variant is the intended production shape for opening-range work.

## Change made

1. **Default strategies → `VWAP_PULLBACK` + `ORB_5M_CROSS`** (`bot-v2-settings.entity.ts`, migration `1789800000000-botV2TrainerOrbCross`). Crossover fires once per range exit instead of every bar outside the range.
2. **`premiumTargetPct` default 22 → 15** (entity + migration). Banks gains inside the spread+commission band before theta erodes them; aligns with replay evidence without adopting the analysis-only 1% scalp.
3. **Pin trainer seed** (`bot-v2-settings.service.ts`): `seedFromChampion` no longer copies champion `strategiesEnabled`; new seeds get the two trainer rules above and `premiumTargetPct: 15`.
4. **Migration** resets existing `v2|SPY|60` rows (SPY, 60s bars, rapid profile not yet applied) to the pinned strategies and 15% target when still at 22%.
5. **`bot-backtest.cli.ts` defaults** aligned to the live entity so future weekly baselines match production knobs.

## Baseline vs candidate replay

Fixture: 2026-09-30 through 2026-10-02. **Sampling: 5s** (`lowFidelity: false`). Sessions before 2026-09-28 were 1-minute snapshots and are not in this window.

| Config | Trades | Win rate | Net P&L | Expectancy |
|--------|--------|----------|---------|------------|
| **Baseline** — `VWAP_PULLBACK` + `ORB_5M`, target 22%, cooldown 2m | 127 | 66.1% | **−$629** | −$4.95 |
| **Candidate** — `VWAP_PULLBACK` + `ORB_5M_CROSS`, target 15%, same exits otherwise | 31 | 90.3% | **+$1,263** | +$40.74 |

Candidate exit mix: **14** `PREMIUM_TARGET` (+$1,241), **15** `TRAIL_STOP` (+$241), **2** `UNDERLYING_STOP` (−$219). Fewer marginal ORB re-entries; more trades reach the closer premium target.

**Fidelity caveat:** replay evaluates exits only on chain snapshots (~5s here). Live tick exits can stop out on touches invisible to replay; coarse runs understate stop-outs and can flatter candidates. These three sessions are 5-second cadence, so the bias is smaller than for pre-2026-09-28 data, but the numbers are still replay estimates, not live P&L.

The capped-settings walk-forward replay reported `actionable=false` (`NO_TRAIN_IMPROVEMENT`); that gate tested a narrow two-key nudge, not this entry-rule change.

## Falsification

- **`v2|SPY|60` net P&L stays negative over the next two full weeks** while trade count drops below ~15/week — would suggest the crossover starved valid continuation entries rather than removing churn.
- **Losses cluster again at 10:00–10:30 on `ORB_5M_CROSS` CALL** with the same −$200+ `UNDERLYING_STOP` profile — would mean the opening-range level, not re-fire logic, is wrong for this tape.
- **Replay with 5s snapshots shows candidate net P&L below baseline** on the next Saturday fixture — would reject the default change even if live looked better (overfitting to three sessions).
- **Pinning strategies causes missed fills the desk expects** (skip census `NO_CONTRACT_MATCH` rises materially without P&L gain) — would argue for restoring champion strategy copy while keeping ORB crossover.
