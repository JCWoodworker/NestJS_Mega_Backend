## Research (mechanism, not slogans)

1. **Session-anchored VWAP fades** — Practitioners treat **session VWAP** (reset at the open) as the fair-value anchor; fades are defined as stretch **beyond a volatility band** with **rejection**, not every tick away from a micro average ([CrossTrade VWAP reversion](https://crosstrade.io/learn/trading-strategies/vwap-reversion); [SPXXL — σ bands as stretch](https://spxxl.com/learn-glossary/vwap)). On 15-second SPXW signal bars, print-count VWAP moves too fast for that definition.

2. **Midday liquidity and momentum** — Intraday volume is **U-shaped**; continuation strategies often lack follow-through when midday participation thins ([Investing.com intraday rhythm / midday chop](https://www.investing.com/analysis/market-timing-how-intraday-rhythms-shape-stock-trades-200667883); [Thinkorswim lunch-hour research](https://tosindicators.com/research/lunch-trading)).

3. **Opening-range break timing** — ORB research commonly treats the **first five minutes** as discovery; acting on the **immediate** post-range break often catches a **false breakout** before the 9:45–10:00 window stabilizes (same practitioner ORB write-ups above; mirrored in prior desk backtests that waited before arming continuation).

4. **Late-day 0DTE calls** — Into the last hour, **theta acceleration** and **pin/gamma** effects dominate short-call convexity; momentum **calls** late session are a different regime from afternoon **puts** that worked this week at **13:00**.

## Change made (and why it targets the failure)

### 1. `VWAP_REVERSION` — session anchor + stricter stretch

- Trainer engine passes **`sessionVwap`** from **1-minute** session bars into strategy evaluation (same math as the trend gate).
- Fades use that anchor, require **1.5× ATR** stretch (was **1×**), and require a **wick through the band** with close still on the stretched side.

Shared helper: `sessionVwapFromCandles` in `bot-strategy.util.ts` (trend gate delegates to it).

### 2. `EMA_MOMENTUM` — lunch blackout + late CALL blackout

- No signals **11:30–13:00 ET** (covers the **12:00** CALL momentum hole).
- No **CALL** signals **14:00–14:45 ET** (covers **14:00** CALL losses without blocking **13:00 PUT** momentum that was profitable).

### 3. `ORB_5M_CROSS` — no fire before 09:45 ET

- Blocks the first-wave false breakouts that feed the **10:00** premium-stop cluster.

### 4. Trainer `tradeWindowEnd` migration

- Sets **`bot_v2_settings.trade_window_end = '14:45'`** for existing **SPXW** rows still on **`15:00`**, matching the documented last-hour entry cut.

No changes to the shared return-on-cost ladder (`lossCutPct`, `protectArmPct`, `protectFloorPct`, `givebackPct`, `tightFromPct`, `tightGivebackPct`), cooldown, kill switch, or `premiumStopPct` / legacy trail fields.

## What would falsify this

1. **`VWAP_REVERSION × PUT`** remains the largest loser next week despite session-anchored **1.5× ATR** fades → disable reversion on the trainer book, not further stretch tuning.
2. **`EMA_MOMENTUM × CALL`** losses **move to 13:00–14:00** with similar dollars → widen lunch/late blackouts or drop the rule on SPXW.
3. **`10:00` bucket** `PREMIUM_STOP` P&L stays worse than other windows while ORB cross count unchanged → revisit ORB definition (range length / confirm bar), not exits.
4. **`rapidScalpReplay` delta vs actual** flips **positive** while net P&L stays negative → reopen exit-ladder work (still not `premiumStopPct` / old trail percents).
