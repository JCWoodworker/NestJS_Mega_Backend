## Research (mechanism, not performance claims)

1. **CrossTrade — [VWAP Reversion](https://crosstrade.io/learn/trading-strategies/vwap-reversion)** — Treats reversion as valid only after price extends **~2 standard deviations / multiple ATRs** from **session** VWAP and shows **rejection** at the extreme; stops sit beyond the trigger, target is VWAP.
2. **TradingView / PinegenAI — [VWAP Mean Reversion with Session Filter](https://www.tradingview.com/script/YnLk5B6z-VWAP-Mean-Reversion-Strategy-with-Session-and-Volume-Filter/)** — Requires stretch in **ATR multiples from session VWAP** plus **declining volume** on the extension (expanding volume suggests trend, not fade).
3. **SPX Lens / Daily Claude — [0DTE setup sequence](https://www.spxlens.com/help/daily-claude)** — For continuation, price must hold the **same side of VWAP** as the trade; tangled structure without VWAP alignment is explicitly a no-trade.

These align with tightening **session-anchored** stretch, demanding **fading participation**, and refusing **flat EMA** “momentum” in chop — without touching `premiumStopPct`, `premiumTargetPct`, or legacy trail percents.

## Change made (shared registry — desk bot + trainer)

1. **`computeSessionTypicalVwap` / `aggregateToMinuteBars`** in `bot-strategy.util.ts` — same session VWAP definition as the trainer trend gate (minute aggregation, volume-weighted or typical-price mean).
2. **`VWAP_REVERSION`** — stretch measured from that session anchor; threshold **1.5× ATR** (was 1×); trigger bar must have **non-increasing** bar volume vs the prior bar (15-second participation proxy).
3. **`EMA_MOMENTUM`** — requires **|EMA9 − EMA21| ≥ 0.12× ATR** before firing; adds `atr` to strategy needs.

No ladder or cooldown changes. No new settings columns.

## What would falsify this

- **`VWAP_REVERSION` trade count and net P&L unchanged** on the next dossier while other rules improve → the session anchor did not reduce the bad fade population; revisit stretch (e.g. 2× ATR) or disable the rule on this book.
- **Green session-day count does not rise** and net P&L on replay is **≤ baseline** once the fixture runs → the chop filter and reversion gates are too tight or wrong lever; restore or retune `MIN_EMA_SPREAD_ATR` / `STRETCH_ATR` with evidence.
