## Research (mechanism, not performance claims)

1. **VWAP mean reversion vs trend regime** — Fading VWAP extensions only makes sense when the session is rotational; on trend days price holds one side of VWAP and fades donate. Classify first, then pick pullback-with-trend vs fade. [LuxAlgo — VWAP mean-reversion vs trend regimes](https://www.luxalgo.com/library/concept/vwap-mean-reversion-vs-trend-regimes/); [Plutux — trend-day filter as the real strategy](https://plutux.ai/resources/trading-systems/intraday-vwap-mean-reversion/rules); [CrossTrade — ADX / opening-range regime skip for reversion](https://crosstrade.io/learn/trading-strategies/vwap-reversion).
2. **Intraday trend strength by clock** — ES tends to trend more cleanly in the **opening** and **late** segments, with a **weaker trend midday** (lunch chop). [Adam Grimes — intraday trend strength by time of day](https://www.adamhgrimes.com/intraday-tendencies-sp-futures/). That matches this week’s dossier: EMA momentum **calls** bleeding at **12:00**, **puts** working into **13:00**.

## Change made (shared strategy code — desk + trainer)

Both edits live in `src/subapps/schwab/bot/strategies/` (registry used by v1 and v2).

1. **`EMA_MOMENTUM`** — Do not evaluate before **12:30 ET** (180 minutes from session open). The rule comment already called it an “afternoon trend rule”; it was firing through lunch, where this book lost **−$1,562** on CALL EMA in the 12:00 bucket alone.
2. **`VWAP_REVERSION`** — Skip when **session range ÷ ATR > 10** on the signal timeframe. On 15s SPXW bars a fixed point cap misfires; a ratio blocks wide-range (trend) sessions while leaving tight chop eligible — aligned with “fade only on balanced days” in the sources above.

Unit tests: `registry.spec.ts` (chop fade still fires, wide-range trend blocked, EMA blocked before 12:30).

No change to `lossCutPct` / protect ladder (capped replay did not clear the bar; rapid-scalp counterfactual argues against exit tightening).
